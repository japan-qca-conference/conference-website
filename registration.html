Paper Development Workshop

The PDW welcomes researchers from different domains who wish to share QCA experiences, exchange ideas, and develop an empirical QCA paper. The workshop uses small roundtables so that paper developers can receive detailed, constructive feedback from QCA experts.

Eligibility and scope

The proposal must describe an empirical study in which QCA is already being applied. A submission may not be accepted if the project is at a stage before applying QCA or if it is purely conceptual rather than an empirical application of QCA.

Required abstract content

Name the file YourSurname_PDWJ26 and explain the QCA-based research design, including:

  1. The research question or questions
  2. The analytical framework
  3. The strategy for collecting raw data and calibrating cases
  4. The reasons for using QCA to address the research gap

Within the 500-word limit, provide detailed information on the data—including the number of cases and conditions—and the empirical findings of the QCA study. Include the title and all authors’ information in the file.

Review and next steps

An expert panel will review the clarity of purpose and methodological rigor. Accepted applicants will be invited to submit a full paper of no more than 8,000 words for presentation at the PDW. A full-paper deadline is not stated in the supplied call.

Travel grant

PhD students who have no or insufficient funding to travel to Tokyo may apply for a travel grant. Grants are expected not to exceed ¥50,000. Further application details will be provided when applicants are notified of the decision regarding their abstract.

Expert Workshop

The EW invites experienced QCA researchers from a variety of disciplinary backgrounds to present, develop, and discuss methodological issues related to QCA. It is a hybrid event, combining in-person and online participation, and is organized as a discussion forum rather than a conventional paper-presentation session.

Eligibility and scope

The main focus of the proposal should be on developing QCA or a related method. Empirical applications may be used to illustrate the methodological issue. The supplied call does not state separate selection or scoring criteria beyond this scope.

Required abstract content

Submit a maximum 500-word abstract as a Word or PDF file. Name the file YourSurname_EWJ26 and include the title and all authors’ information. No additional required abstract sections are stated in the supplied call.

Participation and next steps

Applicants will be notified around 31 August 2026. Accepted presenters will be invited to submit a full paper, although a full paper is not required; no EW paper-length limit is stated. QCA experts may also participate without presenting a paper and should indicate this using the same online form.

Submission data and privacy

This website does not collect or store proposals, names, email addresses, or other submission data. Each application button opens an external Google Form. Review the information requested and any notices shown on the relevant form before submitting.

Please do not email abstract files unless the organizers specifically request this. Questions about the call may be sent to jqca2026@gmail.com.
